<div class="page-kicker">01 / Research collaboration</div>

<section class="editorial-intro lab-intro">
  <div class="module-kicker">People · platforms · information · impact</div>
  <p>I advise and collaborate with students across journalism, communication, informatics, computer science, data science, and statistics.</p>
  <p>Our work examines how people encounter consequential information through search, social platforms, and AI—and how computational methods can measure those processes responsibly.</p>
</section>

<aside class="central-question lab-question">
  <div class="module-kicker">How we work</div>
  <blockquote>Students contribute across the research process: developing concepts and measures, collecting data, validating computational analyses, interpreting results, and writing for publication.</blockquote>
</aside>
